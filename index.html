FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you want to know before starting.

What exactly is an AI calling agent?

It's an AI voice assistant that handles phone calls for your business — answering questions, booking appointments and taking messages in a natural voice, 24 hours a day. It never misses a call, even outside working hours.

How long does a website or app take to build?

It depends on the size of the project. A business website usually takes 1–3 weeks, and an app or AI agent is scoped after a short discovery call so you get an honest timeline before we start.

Do I need a website before getting an AI agent?

Not necessarily — AI agents can work on your existing website, WhatsApp or phone line. But pairing an agent with a new website or app gives you the best results, since everything works as one system.

Will SEO really help my business?

SEO brings you customers who are already searching for what you sell — the highest-intent traffic there is. Combined with a fast website, it compounds over time instead of stopping the moment you stop paying for ads.
